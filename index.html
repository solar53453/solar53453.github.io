Save With Solar

Why Consider Solar Energy?

Electricity tariffs keep rising, and every unit your roof generates is a unit you do not buy. A correctly designed solar system can reduce your dependence on grid power, protect you from tariff increases, and — with batteries — keep your essential loads running during load shedding. Actual savings depend on your consumption, system size and usage pattern, which is why we design around your real electricity bill instead of promising fixed figures.

  • Generate your own electricity from a free, renewable source
  • Reduce monthly bills with net billing on grid-connected systems
  • Optional battery backup for fans, lights and essential appliances
  • Systems designed for homes, offices, shops and commercial buildings

Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Which solar system is suitable for my home?

It depends on your monthly electricity units, roof space and whether you need backup during load shedding. Share your electricity bill with us and we will recommend a suitable system type and capacity.

What is the difference between on-grid and hybrid solar?

An on-grid system connects with the grid and uses net billing to reduce bills — extra units are exported and credited under the current rules — but it shuts down during power outages. A hybrid system also has batteries, so backed-up loads keep running during load shedding.

Can a solar system provide backup during a power outage?

Yes, with a hybrid or off-grid system that includes batteries. The backup covers the loads connected to it, for a duration based on battery capacity.

What factors affect solar installation costs?

System capacity, panel and inverter selection, battery storage, mounting structure, roof condition and wiring requirements all affect the final quotation. A site assessment gives an accurate figure.

How much roof space is required?

As a rough guide, about 100 square feet of shade-free roof per kilowatt of panels. We measure your roof precisely during the site assessment.

Is battery storage necessary?

Only if you need power during load shedding or at night from stored energy. If your goal is only bill reduction and grid supply is reliable, an on-grid system without batteries may suit you.

How can I request a site assessment?

Call us, message us on WhatsApp, or submit the quote form on this website. We will arrange a visit to study your roof, load and requirements.

What maintenance does a solar system require?

Regular panel cleaning, periodic connection and structure checks, and inverter monitoring. We offer cleaning, maintenance and inspection services for existing systems as well.
