Commercial Solar Installation in DHA Lahore

Larger rooftop solar systems for offices, shops, schools and commercial buildings in DHA Lahore, planned around business consumption.

Commercial properties consume most of their electricity during daylight hours, which makes solar a natural fit. We install commercial solar systems in DHA Lahore for offices, retail outlets, schools and other businesses, planned around operating hours and load profile.

Problems This Service Solves

  • High daytime electricity costs affecting business margins
  • Power interruptions disturbing customers and operations
  • Large idle rooftops on commercial buildings
  • Difficulty estimating the right capacity for a business load

Our Solutions & Process

  • Load study based on your business electricity bills
  • System design aligned with daytime operating hours
  • Phased installation planning to reduce disruption to business
  • Testing, commissioning and staff handover on system use

Components & System Options

  • High-capacity panel arrays for large rooftops
  • Three-phase inverter options for commercial loads
  • Industrial-grade mounting and cabling
  • Monitoring options to track generation and performance

Factors That Influence Design & Cost

  • Business electricity consumption and tariff category
  • Roof area and structural condition
  • Operating hours and peak load timings
  • Net metering and regulatory requirements for your connection

Why Professional Commercial Solar Installation Matters

Commercial systems are larger and more complex than home systems. Proper load study and engineering prevent underperformance and keep the installation safe for staff, customers and equipment.
