Solar System Inspection in DHA Lahore

Complete health inspection of existing solar systems in DHA Lahore — ideal before buying a property or when generation drops.

A solar system inspection gives you a clear, honest picture of what your system is doing and what it needs. We inspect existing installations across DHA Lahore — for owners seeing falling output, and for buyers checking a system before purchasing a property.

Problems This Service Solves

  • Generation lower than expected with no clear reason
  • Buying a house with an existing solar system
  • A system installed by another company that was never verified
  • Safety concerns about wiring, structure or earthing

Our Solutions & Process

  • Full visual and electrical inspection of panels, structure and wiring
  • Generation testing against system capacity
  • Inverter settings and battery health review
  • Clear report of faults, risks and recommended actions

Components & System Options

  • Panel condition and hotspot screening
  • Structure, mounting and roof sealing checks
  • Cable, breaker and earthing inspection
  • Performance summary in plain language

Factors That Influence Design & Cost

  • System size, age and documentation available
  • Accessibility of roof and electrical points
  • Whether batteries are part of the system
  • Depth of report required

Why Professional Solar System Inspection Matters

You cannot manage what you have not measured. A proper inspection replaces guesswork with facts, whether you are fixing a problem or valuing a property.

Frequently Asked Questions

What will I receive after the inspection?

A clear summary of your system's condition, any faults found, safety concerns and recommended next steps with priorities.

Can you inspect a system before I buy a house?

Yes — a pre-purchase inspection tells you whether the installed system works properly and what it may cost to fix.

How long does an inspection take?

Most home systems are inspected within a single visit. Larger commercial systems may take longer.
