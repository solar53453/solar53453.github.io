Battery storage installation for hybrid and off-grid systems in DHA Lahore — lithium and tubular options sized to your backup needs.

Batteries decide how useful your solar system is after sunset and during load shedding. We install lithium and tubular battery storage for homes and businesses in DHA Lahore, sized around the loads you actually need to keep running.

Problems This Service Solves

  • Backup ending too soon during long load shedding
  • Old batteries that no longer hold charge
  • Confusion between lithium and tubular battery options

Our Solutions & Process

  • Backup load and duration calculation before recommending capacity
  • Honest comparison of lithium and tubular options for your budget
  • Installation with correct cables, breakers and ventilation
  • Inverter charging settings matched to your battery type

Components & System Options

  • Lithium battery packs with built-in battery management
  • Tubular batteries for budget-friendly backup
  • Battery cables, breakers and safety fittings
  • Battery stands or enclosures where required

Factors That Influence Design & Cost

  • Loads you want on backup and their wattage
  • Backup hours required

Frequently Asked Questions

Lithium or tubular — which is better for me?

Lithium lasts longer and charges faster but costs more upfront. Tubular is economical for moderate backup. We compare both against your usage and budget.

How long do solar batteries last?

Life depends on battery type, depth of discharge and maintenance. We explain realistic expectations for each option before you decide.

Can batteries be added to my existing system?

Usually yes, if your inverter is hybrid or off-grid compatible. We check your current setup first.
