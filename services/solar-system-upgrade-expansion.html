Solar System Upgrade and Expansion in DHA Lahore

Add panels, a bigger inverter or more battery capacity to your existing solar system — including systems installed by other companies.

Families grow, appliances increase and bills change — solar systems often need to grow too. Our upgrade and expansion service in DHA Lahore adds panels, replaces an undersized inverter or increases battery storage on your existing system, including systems originally installed by another company.

Problems This Service Solves

  • Electricity usage grown beyond what your system generates
  • New appliances or an extra floor added to the house
  • An inverter that is too small for your current panels or load
  • Battery backup that no longer lasts through load shedding

Our Solutions & Process

  • Full inspection of your existing system and generation
  • Compatibility check between old and new equipment
  • Panel, inverter or battery upgrade with proper configuration
  • Post-upgrade testing so the whole system works as one

Components & System Options

  • Additional solar panels matched to your existing array
  • Larger hybrid or on-grid inverter where required
  • Extra battery capacity for longer backup
  • Updated protection devices and cabling

Factors That Influence Design & Cost

  • Your existing system size, age and condition
  • Available roof space for more panels
  • Inverter capacity and compatibility
  • How much your consumption has increased

Why Professional Solar System Upgrade and Expansion Matters

Mixing old and new equipment without a compatibility check causes faults and lost generation. A planned upgrade protects your original investment and makes the expanded system perform as a single unit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you upgrade a system installed by another company?

Yes. We first inspect the existing installation, then recommend what can be reused and what should be added or replaced.

Do I need a new inverter to add more panels?

Only if your current inverter is already at its capacity limit. We check this during inspection and tell you honestly.

Will expansion affect my net billing?

A capacity change may need your application details updated. We explain what is required for your connection during the assessment.
