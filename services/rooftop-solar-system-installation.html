Rooftop Solar System Installation in DHA Lahore

Complete rooftop solar systems for houses and buildings in DHA Lahore, including structure, panels, inverter and wiring.

A rooftop solar system combines panels, mounting structure, inverter and wiring into one complete package on your roof. We deliver complete rooftop installations in DHA Lahore for houses and commercial buildings, planned around roof condition and your electricity needs.

Problems This Service Solves

  • Wanting one complete solution instead of arranging parts separately
  • Uncertainty about whether your roof can support a solar system
  • Concerns about roof leaks after installation
  • Confusion about structure types for different roof styles

Our Solutions & Process

  • Roof inspection for strength, space, direction and shading
  • Structure selection suited to your roof type
  • Complete supply and installation of panels, inverter and wiring
  • Waterproof sealing of all mounting points and final testing

Components & System Options

  • Solar panels and mounting structure as one engineered set
  • Inverter and batteries according to system type
  • Complete DC and AC wiring with protection
  • Earthing and lightning protection considerations

Factors That Influence Design & Cost

  • Roof type — flat, sloped or elevated structure
  • Roof condition and load-bearing capacity
  • Shading from nearby buildings, trees or water tanks
  • System capacity and future expansion space

Why Professional Rooftop Solar System Installation Matters

A rooftop system is only as good as its structure and sealing. Professional roof assessment and mounting protect both the solar investment and the building underneath it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can solar be installed on any roof?

Most flat and sloped roofs can support solar with the right structure. A roof inspection confirms suitability before installation.

Will the structure withstand strong wind?

Structures are fixed and braced for local wind conditions. Correct anchoring is a standard part of our installation.

How much roof space does a system need?

Space depends on capacity. As a rough guide, each kilowatt needs about 100 square feet of shade-free roof — we measure exactly during assessment.
