Solar Panel Repair in DHA Lahore

Diagnosis and repair of damaged, underperforming or faulty solar panels for existing systems in DHA Lahore.

When solar panels crack, develop hotspots or stop producing as expected, generation and savings drop quietly. We inspect and repair faulty panels in DHA Lahore, and advise honestly when replacement is the better option.

Problems This Service Solves

  • A sudden drop in system generation
  • Cracked or physically damaged panels
  • Hotspots, discolouration or burnt marks on panels
  • Loose connections or damaged panel cabling

Our Solutions & Process

  • On-site inspection and generation testing of the array
  • Identification of faulty panels and connection issues
  • Repair of cabling, connectors and mounting where possible
  • Clear advice when a panel is beyond repair and needs replacement

Components & System Options

  • Panel testing with generation and voltage checks
  • Replacement connectors, cabling and junction fittings
  • Mounting and sealing repairs
  • Compatible replacement panels where required

Factors That Influence Design & Cost

  • Age and condition of the existing panels
  • Type of damage — electrical, physical or weather-related
  • Availability of matching replacement panels
  • Whether the fault affects one panel or the whole string

Why Professional Solar Panel Repair Matters

One faulty panel can drag down an entire string of panels. Proper diagnosis finds the real fault instead of guesswork, and safe repair protects the rest of your system.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if a panel is faulty?

Signs include falling generation, visible cracks, hotspots or burn marks. Our inspection confirms the fault with proper testing.

Can a cracked panel be repaired?

Minor connection faults can be repaired, but cracked glass usually means replacement. We inspect first and advise honestly.

Do you repair systems installed by others?

Yes. We diagnose and repair existing systems regardless of who installed them.
