Frequently Asked Questions

How often should panels be cleaned?

In Lahore's dusty environment, cleaning every two to four weeks in dry season keeps output healthy. We suggest a schedule based on your location.

Does cleaning really improve generation?

Yes — heavy dust can cut output noticeably. The dirtier the panels, the bigger the improvement after cleaning.

Can I clean panels myself with a pipe?

Hard water and rough cloth can leave mineral marks and micro-scratches. If you clean yourself, use soft cloth and clean water, early morning.
